WhatsApp clamps the description to two lines — the reason the copy is 109 characters rather than the 197 of the longer draft. This is the surface 4a was actually written for: a link sent to a doctor or a family member.

iMessage shows the image and title and drops the description entirely. That is why “NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE” is burned into the card itself rather than left to the text — on this surface the card is the only thing carrying it.
