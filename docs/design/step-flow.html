Design 3 · complete flow · 44 screens · matched to PLAN v25

Every screen, one question at a time

The Step design carried all the way through — setup, the everyday path, every screen that warns or refuses, and the records. The rule throughout: one decision per screen, and nothing else visible while a number is being typed.

Keypad fixed. The earlier version had no zero and no delete key — a real bug, not a stylisation. Both are in now, along with the decimal point that carbohydrate entry needs and blood sugar does not.
